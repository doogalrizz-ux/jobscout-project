How to use this (and how to use it with AI)
  1. Pick your roles, location and salary on the left. Your choices are saved in this browser.
  2. Come back each morning. Jobs marked NEW appeared since your last visit.
  3. Apply takes you straight to the employer's own posting. Show description reads the full job description right here, and Copy description puts it on your clipboard for your resume or cover letter.
  4. ☆ Save keeps a job on your Saved tab.
  5. Using ChatGPT or Claude? Click Export for AI to download your current matches (up to 100) with full descriptions. Upload that file to your AI chat with your resume and use the prompt below to rank them.
